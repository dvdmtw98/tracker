> [!IMPORTANT] Summary
> **11/22/63** follows Jake Epping, a high-school teacher who discovers a way to travel into the past. Given the chance to change history, he embarks on a years-long journey to prevent one of the most significant events of the twentieth century. But as he becomes immersed in the world of the late 1950s and early 1960s, he learns that the past may not want to be changed.
