> [!IMPORTANT] Summary
> **8th Confession** follows Detective Lindsay Boxer and the Women’s Murder Club as they investigate two disturbing cases in San Francisco. One involves the murder of a wealthy socialite couple, while the other centers around the brutal death of a homeless man known as “Bagman Jesus.” As the investigations grow darker, the group is pulled into a web of secrets, class divisions, and hidden violence beneath the city’s polished surface.
