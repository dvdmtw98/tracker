> [!IMPORTANT] Summary
> **Human Acts** follows the lives of ordinary people connected to the 1980 Gwangju Uprising in South Korea, showing how violence and political brutality continue to shape people long after the events themselves are over. Through multiple perspectives, the novel explores grief, memory, trauma, and what it means to remain human in the face of unimaginable cruelty.
