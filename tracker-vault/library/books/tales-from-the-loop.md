> [!IMPORTANT] Summary
> In an alternate Sweden of the 1980s, beneath the quiet countryside of Mälaröarna lies the Loop a vast experimental particle accelerator built to unlock the secrets of the universe.
> 
> But the Loop has changed more than science.
> 
> Around it, children grow up among abandoned robots, impossible machines, strange creatures, and fragments of technology they barely understand. For them, the extraordinary becomes part of everyday life: a rusting machine in a field, a mysterious structure beneath the earth, a creature glimpsed at the edge of the woods.
> 
> Told through haunting illustrations and fragments of remembered stories, _Tales from the Loop_ transforms an ordinary Scandinavian childhood into something strange, nostalgic, and quietly unsettling a world where the familiar and the impossible exist side by side.
