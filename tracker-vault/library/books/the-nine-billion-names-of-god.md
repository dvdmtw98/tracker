> [!IMPORTANT] Summary
> **The Nine Billion Names of God** is a science fiction short story collection by Arthur C. Clarke, anchored by the famous title story in which a group of Tibetan monks hires computer engineers to help them list every possible name of God according to their ancient belief that the universe’s purpose is tied to this task. Alongside this and other tales, the collection weaves together ideas about technology, humanity, and the unknown in thought-provoking and imaginative ways that stay with you long after you finish reading.
