> [!IMPORTANT] Summary
> Three tons of Saddam Hussein’s gold. A seemingly perfect robbery. Two of Nick Stone’s former SAS comrades are betrayed, and the job ends in murder.
> 
> Stone sets out to find the person responsible, but what begins as a hunt for revenge soon leads him into a far deeper conspiracy involving corruption, powerful interests, and a Russian investigative journalist who may hold the key to the truth. As the pieces fall into place, Stone finds himself drawn into a deadly race where trust is scarce and the stakes keep rising.
