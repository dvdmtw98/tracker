> [!IMPORTANT] Summary
> **The Boy, the Mole, the Fox and the Horse** is a gentle, illustrated story about a lonely boy who meets three new friends as he walks through the world. As they travel together, they talk about the things that matter most in life like kindness, courage, hope and friendship. Each page shows beautiful drawings and wise thoughts that help readers think about life in a hopeful and warm way.
