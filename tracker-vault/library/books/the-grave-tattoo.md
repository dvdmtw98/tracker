> [!IMPORTANT] Summary
> **The Grave Tattoo** by Val McDermid is a layered mystery set in England’s Lake District that begins when heavy summer rains expose a strangely tattooed body in a peat bog that some believe could be tied to an old local legend about a famous mutineer. Jane Gresham, a Wordsworth scholar with deep ties in the region, becomes determined to uncover the truth behind the discovery and to find a long-lost manuscript that may connect history, poetry, and the unexplained. As Jane follows leads from rural villages to academic archives, her intellectual hunt gradually draws her into a dangerous and complex web of secrets where the past still holds power over the present.
