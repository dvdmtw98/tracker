> [!IMPORTANT] Summary
> **The Willows** is a classic supernatural tale about two friends on a canoe trip down the River Danube who find themselves camped on a remote, willow-covered island where the natural world seems to have a life and will of its own. As strange and unsettling events unfold around them, an eerie sense of unseen forces and unexplained phenomena grows until they begin to question their place in a wilderness that feels alive and potentially hostile. The story blends atmospheric horror with a deep sense of mystery that lingers long after the final page.
