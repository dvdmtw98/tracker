> [!IMPORTANT] summary
> **Malgudi Days** is a collection of short stories set in the fictional town of Malgudi in South India. Each story follows a different character, from schoolboys and shopkeepers to teachers and wanderers, and shows a small part of their everyday lives.
> 
> The book captures ordinary moments with warmth, humor, and quiet insight. Through simple events and personal struggles, it reflects human nature, relationships, and the rhythms of small-town life. Malgudi may be imaginary, but it feels real, like a place shaped by the people who live in it.
