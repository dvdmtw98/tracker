> [!IMPORTANT] Summary
> A deadly sickness has taken over London. Everyone older than a teenager has either died or turned into something violent and dangerous. The only people left are children, and they have to survive on their own.
> 
> Small groups of kids hide in buildings, searching for food and trying to stay safe. The biggest threat comes from the infected adults, who roam the streets hunting them.
> 
> When rumors of a safer place begin to spread, some of the kids decide to leave their shelter and cross the city. But the journey is risky, and they quickly learn that survival is not just about fighting the infected, but also about trust, fear, and the choices they make.
