> [!IMPORTANT] Summary
> As an elderly man begins to lose his grip on his memories, he retreats into a world where the past and present blend together. Through conversations with his grandson and reflections on the people he loves, he struggles to hold on to the moments that have shaped his life.
>
> A moving and deeply personal novella about family, memory, love, and the fear of forgetting, it explores what remains of us when our memories begin to fade.
