> On a ship traveling from New York to Buenos Aires, passengers discover that the world chess champion is aboard. After a series of tense games, a mysterious stranger unexpectedly challenges him, revealing an extraordinary understanding of chess and a deeply troubled past.
> What begins as a simple contest slowly turns into a psychological battle about isolation, obsession, and the fragile limits of the human mind.
