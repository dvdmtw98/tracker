> [!IMPORTANT] Summary
> **Remina** follows a young woman who suddenly finds herself at the center of worldwide attention after an unknown planet is discovered and named after her. But the excitement quickly turns to terror when the mysterious planet begins moving through space on an inexplicable course, leaving destruction in its wake.
> 
> As humanity struggles to understand what is approaching Earth, fear and paranoia begin to spread. A bleak, grotesque cosmic-horror story about an incomprehensible threat and the terrifying consequences of humanity's response to it.
