> **Tombs** is a collection of Junji Ito horror stories built around strange towns, unsettling transformations, grotesque neighbors, and inexplicable phenomena. From a community filled with countless tombstones to a girl afflicted with a disturbing condition and a house with only a single window, the stories gradually turn ordinary situations into deeply unsettling nightmares.
