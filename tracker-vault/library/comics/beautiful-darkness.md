> [!IMPORTANT] Summary
> **Beautiful Darkness** follows Aurora and a group of tiny people who suddenly find themselves homeless in a vast and unfamiliar forest. As they struggle to survive and build a new community, the dangers around them and the tensions between them begin to expose a much darker side of human nature.
> 
> Beautifully illustrated in delicate watercolors, this is a twisted fairy tale that gradually turns unsettling, exploring survival, cruelty, jealousy, and the fragile idea of civilization.
